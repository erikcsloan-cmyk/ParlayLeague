🏈 Parlay Pick 'Em Family League

Quick Guide - everything you need to know to get picking

Logging In

First time: pick your name from the dropdown, then verify with your phone number. Check "Remember me on this device" and you'll never have to log in again on that phone or computer - it'll open straight to the app.

🏠 Home

Your landing page. Cards show up here when there's something you should know:

  • Reminder - if you haven't finished this week's picks yet.
  • Runback vote - a live Yes / No card while a vote is open (see Runback below).
  • Parlay placed - once the week's Parlay is in, a card shows who placed it, how many legs, the odds, and what it pays if it hits. If a runback gets placed, that card replaces it.
  • Last week's results - a table of how everyone did the week before (record, accuracy, and season rank), with a 🏆 next to the week's top finisher. It refreshes every Tuesday morning.
  • Group updates - the weekly consensus picks and runback results, plus any note someone posts to the group. You can dismiss ones you've read, and remove notes you posted yourself.

New items are highlighted until you've seen them.

🏈 Games

This is home base - make your picks here each week.

  • Picks are blind. You won't see anyone else's pick for a game until you've submitted your own - no bandwagoning off whoever picks first.
  • Picks open Tuesday, close Wednesday by default (occasionally a specific week's window gets extended - you'll see the exact open/close dates right on screen).
  • Once a game's final, your pick shows: green (you won)   red (you lost)   gold (the game was a tie)

🔥 Parlay

This is the group's "consensus" - whichever team got the most individual picks that week. Shows the pick, the result once the game's final, and everyone's individual dot for that game.

While the week's still in progress, you'll see a running "parlay potential" banner showing what the group's current picks would pay if they all hit, priced off the latest odds.

🎉 Perfect pick: if every single person picks the same winning team on a game, you'll get a little gold celebration banner.
🙈 Group whiff: and if every single person picks wrong on a game... you'll hear about that too.

When the group's Parlay is actually placed, it's marked on this screen and everyone gets a text and a card on Home with the details.

🏆 Winning Parlay: once every game in the week is final, a banner shows what the parlay would have paid if every single pick had been the actual winning team - priced at the real closing odds. A fun "what could've been," whether or not the real parlay actually hit.

🔁 Runback

If the Parlay's pick in the first game of the week (usually Thursday night) loses, the group can vote to "run it back" - place a new Parlay on the remaining games.

  • When the game goes final, a Yes / No card appears on Home and you get a text.
  • The vote closes at 11:59 PM the day after that first game (Friday night for a Thursday game).
  • It passes when more than half of the league votes Yes. If the deadline arrives without a majority, there's no runback.
  • As soon as it's decided, everyone gets a text - "Runback vote passed" or "Runback vote failed" - and it's posted on Home.
  • If it passes and the runback is placed, a card on Home shows who's in and the odds.

Text replies (RB YES / RB NO) will work as an alternative to the Home card once the league's text replies are switched on. Until then, vote on Home.

⚖️ Ties

Only shows up when the group's votes split evenly on a game. You'll get a text if this happens - just come vote again on the specific tied game(s). If it ties again, you'll get prompted for another round.

The fun part: if the group genuinely can't agree even after a re-vote, whoever had the best individual record last week gets to make the final call (or the season leader, if last week doesn't have a clear #1). Everyone else won't even see this option - only whoever's earned it.

📊 Table

Ranked by average weekly accuracy (not just total correct picks) - so a great week and a rough week balance out fairly, rather than one bad week early in the season tanking you forever.

It opens on All Weeks (the season so far). Pick a single week from the dropdown to see just that week; the app remembers the one you chose.

👤 Profile

Set your nickname and choose which texts you want. Everything saves from this one screen.

  • Text me - the master switch. Off means no texts at all.
  • Week's consensus picks - the group's picks once everyone's in.
  • Tie-break alerts and Tie-break results - when a tie is found, and when it's still tied or resolved.
  • Runback votes - when a vote opens, and whether it passed or failed.
  • Week open & Parlay placed - Tuesday's "picks are open" text, plus the Parlay and Runback placed texts.
  • Pick reminders - up to three of your own: choose a day (Tuesday to Thursday) and a time. They go only to you, and only if you haven't finished your picks.

Texts go out automatically when...

  • Everyone's picks are in for the week - you'll get the group's consensus for every game
  • A tie is found, is still tied after a re-vote, or gets resolved
  • A runback vote opens, and when it passes or fails
  • The Parlay (or a runback) is placed
  • You haven't picked yet - only if you've set up your own reminders

You can switch any of these on or off yourself on the Profile screen.

✨ A Few Fun Details

  • Everyone can set a nickname that shows up everywhere instead of your login name
  • The app knows the difference between preseason and regular season - preseason games are fun to pick, but don't count toward real Standings
  • The app opens quickly - Home, Games, Parlay and the Table are prepared ahead of time, so they're ready when you tap them
  • Tied games are always called out clearly - no more confusing red "loss" coloring on an actual tie
